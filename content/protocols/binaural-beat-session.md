## Overview

A binaural beat session uses stereo headphones to deliver slightly different frequencies to each ear. The brain perceives a third "beat" at the difference frequency, which can entrain neural oscillations toward target states (theta for meditation, alpha for relaxation, delta for deep rest). This is a technology-mediated protocol for altering brainwave patterns without substances.

## Materials

- Stereo headphones (not speakers; binaural effect requires channel separation)
- Audio source with calibrated binaural beat tracks
- Quiet, comfortable space for lying down or sitting
- Optional: eye mask to reduce visual input
- Optional: timer or pre-programmed session length

## Procedure

1. Select a binaural beat track targeting your desired state:
   - Delta (0.5-4 Hz): Deep sleep, unconscious processing
   - Theta (4-8 Hz): Meditation, hypnagogic imagery, memory
   - Alpha (8-13 Hz): Relaxed alertness, calm focus
   - Beta (13-30 Hz): Active thinking, concentration
   - Gamma (30+ Hz): Peak awareness, insight states
2. Put on headphones and ensure proper stereo orientation (L/R correct).
3. Lie down or sit in a comfortable position; close eyes.
4. Breathe slowly and allow attention to settle on the audio.
5. Do not strain to "hear" the beat; let it work subliminally.
6. Remain still for the duration of the track.
7. When complete, pause before standing; reorient gradually.

## Notes on Intensity

Intensity is subtle but cumulative. Single sessions may produce mild relaxation; repeated practice deepens entrainment capacity. Some individuals are more responsive than others; efficacy varies.

## Expected Effects

- Shifts in mental state corresponding to target frequency
- Time distortion (sessions feel shorter than clock time)
- Hypnagogic imagery during theta sessions
- Improved sleep onset with delta protocols
- Enhanced focus and reduced anxiety with alpha protocols
- Occasional vivid ideation or insight during longer sessions
