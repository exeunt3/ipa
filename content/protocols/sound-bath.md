## Overview

A facilitator creates a bed of slowly shifting tones using singing bowls, chimes, and other resonant instruments. Participants lie down or sit comfortably with eyes closed and allow the sound to fill their awareness.

## Steps

1. Prepare a comfortable space with mats, blankets, and optional eye masks. Dim the lights.
2. Invite participants to settle in, take a few slow breaths, and set a gentle intention for the session.
3. Begin layering resonant sounds, pacing the volume to avoid sudden spikes. Allow long, overlapping tones.
4. Maintain a steady flow for the planned duration, periodically reminding participants they can adjust posture if needed.
5. Fade the sound gradually. When silence returns, prompt a few deep breaths before slowly sitting up.

## Integration

Offer time for quiet reflection or journaling. Encourage hydration and light movement to reorient before leaving the space.
