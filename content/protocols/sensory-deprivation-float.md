## Overview

The sensory deprivation float is a protocol of radical environmental reduction. By suspending the body in skin-temperature salt water within a lightless, soundless enclosure, the ordinary stream of sensory input is minimized to near-zero. What remains is the body's own signals: heartbeat, breath, the faint hum of the nervous system. The practice cultivates proprioceptive dissolution, time distortion, and access to hypnagogic states without sleep.

## Materials

- Float tank or isolation pod with Epsom salt solution (density sufficient for effortless buoyancy)
- Complete darkness (no light leaks)
- Soundproofing or white noise dampening
- Temperature regulation (water and air at skin temperature, ~34-35°C)
- Shower facilities for pre- and post-float rinse

## Procedure

1. Shower to remove oils and prepare skin for salt immersion.
2. Enter the tank and close the lid or door completely.
3. Lie back and allow the salt solution to support you without effort.
4. Release muscular tension systematically, starting from extremities.
5. Avoid deliberate movement; let small oscillations settle naturally.
6. Do not attempt to "achieve" anything; allow experience to unfold.
7. When session ends (often signaled by music or light), exit slowly.
8. Shower to remove salt residue; hydrate.

## Notes on Intensity

Intensity is inversely proportional to sensory input. The less you do, the more emerges. Early sessions may involve restlessness or boredom; these are thresholds. Repeated practice deepens access to non-ordinary states without pharmacological intervention.

## Expected Effects

- Loss of body boundary perception
- Time dilation or collapse
- Hypnagogic imagery and ideation
- Deep muscular and nervous system relaxation
- Insight states or creative problem-solving
- Occasional experiences of vast space or ego softening
