## Overview

The breathwork circle is a group protocol using sustained rhythmic breathing to induce non-ordinary states. By maintaining elevated respiratory rate for extended periods, participants access somatic release, emotional catharsis, and altered consciousness without external substances. The practice draws on traditions including holotropic breathwork, rebirthing, and pranayama intensives.

## Materials

- Padded floor space (yoga mats, blankets)
- Pillows for head and knee support
- Tissues and water accessible
- Trained facilitator or experienced guide
- Optional: evocative music playlist
- Optional: eye masks or blindfolds

## Roles

- **Breathers**: Participants engaging in the breathing protocol
- **Sitters**: Support persons who remain in ordinary consciousness to assist breathers
- **Facilitator**: Guides the session, manages timing, responds to emergencies

## Procedure

1. Gather in a circle; establish container through verbal framing and consent.
2. Breathers lie supine; sitters position nearby.
3. Begin with slow, deep breathing to establish rhythm.
4. Facilitator cues acceleration: continuous connected breath, no pause between inhale and exhale.
5. Maintain elevated breathing rate for 20-45 minutes.
6. Sitters provide grounding touch or verbal reassurance as needed.
7. Facilitator cues gradual deceleration and return to normal breathing.
8. Allow 15-30 minutes of silent integration before movement.
9. Conclude with verbal sharing circle (optional but recommended).

## Notes on Intensity

Intensity escalates with duration and depth of breathing. Physical sensations (tetany, tingling, temperature shifts) are common and generally benign. Emotional release (crying, laughing, vocalization) should be supported, not suppressed. The protocol is contraindicated for those with cardiovascular conditions, pregnancy, or severe trauma without professional support.

## Expected Effects

- Tingling, numbness, or tetany in extremities
- Emotional release: grief, joy, anger, fear
- Somatic memories or body-held tension surfacing
- Visions, imagery, or dream-like states
- Sense of energetic movement or release
- Post-session clarity, lightness, or fatigue
- Deepened connection with co-participants
