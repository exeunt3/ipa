## Overview

A four-piece band (guitar, bass, drums, vocals) meets to rehearse a short set. The emphasis is on consistent tempo, clear cues, and balanced volume so everyone can hear and respond.

## Steps

1. Set up instruments, check cables, and run a quick line check to balance levels.
2. Warm up with a familiar song at moderate volume to align timing and blend.
3. Work through the planned set list, pausing after each song to note fixes.
4. Revisit trouble spots, breaking down transitions or harmonies as needed.
5. End with a full run-through of the set at performance intensity to lock in muscle memory.

## Integration

Share quick feedback, capture rehearsal notes, and schedule the next session. Save any recordings for review by the group.
