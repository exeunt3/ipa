## Report (Bassist)

My role felt less like playing notes and more like maintaining pressure. When the groove held, everything else floated on top of it effortlessly. When it didn’t, the whole structure felt fragile.

There was very little internal commentary. Decisions happened through the body—through tension in the hands and forearms—rather than thought. Afterward, I felt unusually calm, like my nervous system had been reset by repetition.
