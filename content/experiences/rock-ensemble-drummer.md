## Report (Drummer)

There’s a point where time collapses into a loop. Bars stop being counted and instead feel like a surface you’re skating on. When it’s working, the band feels slightly ahead of me and slightly behind me at the same time.

Physical exhaustion sharpened rather than dulled my awareness. Small deviations in bass timing became obvious without thinking about them. When the session ended, stopping felt stranger than playing—as if motion had become the default state.
