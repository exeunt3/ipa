## Report

At first the sound was intrusive. I wanted to locate its source, to orient toward it. That impulse faded as overlapping tones made directionality meaningless.

There was a brief phase of unease, like my usual mental posture had nothing to hold onto. After that, the sensation stabilized into something neutral and wide. My breathing synchronized to the slow pulsing of the sound without effort.

Afterward, ordinary noises felt sharper and more individuated, as if my hearing had been recalibrated rather than dulled.
