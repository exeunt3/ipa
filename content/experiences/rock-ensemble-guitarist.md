## Report (Guitarist)

Once the rhythm section locked in, my hands stopped feeling like they were being directed. I wasn’t choosing notes so much as noticing where my fingers already were.

Mistakes didn’t register as errors unless they broke the groove. When they did, the discomfort was immediate and physical, like slipping on ice. The moment I tried to “correct” consciously, things tightened up. Letting go restored coherence.

The most intense moments weren’t loud—they were when everything thinned out and each sound felt exposed but supported.
