## Report

About ten minutes in, the sound stopped feeling like something I was listening to. It felt more like the room had acquired thickness. My body became hard to locate precisely; limbs felt present but unimportant.

Time did not disappear, but it stopped progressing in a way I could track. There were no images, no memories, no emotional storyline—just a sense of being suspended inside vibration. When the sound thinned out at the end, I noticed resistance to returning. Silence felt abrupt and almost architectural.

The most notable effect was the absence of interpretation. Nothing needed to mean anything.
