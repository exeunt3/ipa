This font is based on the inimitable style of Ralph Steadman,
but of course I tried to imitate it anyway. This font includes
several 'splatter' effects that are outlined below to help new users
most take advantage of this typeset's unique look. All art is
done by me on Drawing paper with black ink and pen nubs of varying
types.
----------

# ' * + - / all create type one splatters
= ? \ ^ ` { } all create type two splatters
to underline any word or phrase, press _ after it.
